How the transformation works

01

Map — no rip-and-replace

We inventory what you already run: the monitoring tools, the telemetry, the alert flow, the on-call rotation. Nothing gets uninstalled. The transformation starts from your stack, not from a green field.

02

Correlate — the Proof Run

Ingestion and correlation plug into your existing sources, and your own history runs through them. The Proof Run report is the deliverable: your noise, collapsed, with the repeat offenders named. You see the conversion before you pay for it.

03

Convert — and hand over the keys

The AIOps operation stands up on the Suite platform: runbooks for the repeats, guardrails that keep consequential actions behind human approval, and your team trained on the new loop. Then we leave. The operation is yours — that’s the point of a transformation.

What changes, what doesn’t

Doesn’t change

Your tools. Your team. Your data. Your on-call rotation. AIOpsify converts the operation; it doesn’t replace the organization.

Changes

One incident instead of 47 alerts. A root cause instead of a guess. A runbook instead of a 3 a.m. scramble. And a record — every incident logged, so the next one starts from evidence instead of memory.
